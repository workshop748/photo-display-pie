==================================================
PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS (PRD)
==================================================

1. PURPOSE  [OK]
A heartfelt gift that Eryss will love and use for a
long time. Because it is meant for long use, it has to
be reliable.

2. SUCCESS MOMENT  [OK]
Developer acceptance test:
  Jacob logs in, uploads several photos, and sees them
  shown on the monitor as an authenticated user.
Customer moment:
  On December 25, Eryss opens the gift, logs in to the
  device from her phone, and uploads an image that is
  displayed within 5 minutes.

3. GOALS  [OK]
G1 (S1) Upload and display a photo from a phone; the
  photo shall be displayed within 5 minutes.
G2 (S2) When a power outage occurs, the device
  remembers the last image. When power is restored,
  the last image shall be presented within 10 minutes.
G3 (S5) Images shall only be changed by logged-in
  users once they have fully authenticated.

4. MVP FEATURES (must work by Dec 25)  [OK]
- Log in to the upload page
- Upload images
- Rotate images on a default or user-set timer
- On boot, the slideshow starts right away on the
  last displayed image
- Shuffle (random order)
- Hide / remove photos
- SSH fix path over WireGuard (S6)

5. V2 (after Dec 25)  [OK]
- Update chain (CI/CD over-the-air updates)
- Monitor speaker / music station support
- Backup of images and configuration
- Optimized recovery
- Other user access / admin roles
- Priority images
- HTTPS (see AR-1)
- Login and heartbeat notifications; email + reboot
  if WireGuard is down (former R23)
- HEIC conversion on the Pi (only if A-1 fails)
- Encrypted photo storage using the Pi 5's
  device-unique OTP key + secure boot (see AR-2)

6. SUCCESS CRITERIA  [OK]
C1 -> G1 (S1): Connect to the device's upload page,
  upload an image, and have it displayed in under
  5 minutes.
C2 -> G2 (S2): During the image rotation, unplug the
  power and wait 10 seconds. Plug it back in; the
  device boots and returns to the last image within
  10 minutes.
C3 -> G3 (S5): Go to the upload page as a user who
  isn't logged in, try to upload an image, and
  confirm the image was not uploaded.

TECH STACK (decided)
==================================================
- App: Rust + Axum, behind Nginx (reverse proxy)
- Images: Rust `image` crate (decode/re-encode)
- Build: cross-compile to aarch64 on the
  workstation (cross or cargo-zigbuild)
- OS: RHEL-family. AlmaLinux supports the Pi 5.
  [Q] Which Pi model? Confirms the OS choice.

==================================================
THREAT MODEL (lightweight)
==================================================
Data flow:
  Phone -> home Wi-Fi -> Nginx -> Axum app
  -> SD card -> display

Trust boundaries (where untrusted data crosses):
  TB-1 Network: anything from the Wi-Fi is
       untrusted. Nginx passes upload bytes through
       unchanged, so uploads stay untrusted until the
       Axum app validates them.
       Covered by: R1, R2, R5a-c, R17-R19, AR-1
  TB-2 Physical: someone holding the SD card.
       Covered by: R15, AR-2, disposal procedure
  TB-3 Admin: WireGuard + SSH (authenticated).
       Covered by: R21, R22

  Test early: the Photos picker, the Files app, and a

  If it fails: HEIC conversion moves from v2 into the
  MVP (one more untrusted-file parser to keep

  Status: [TODO] not yet tested

R2 | S1 | shall | Reject files larger than 25 MB.
   Verify: test with an oversized file  [OK]

   Verify: demo  [OK]

   Verify: timed test  [OK]

R5b | S1 | shall | Give stored files server-created

    Verify: upload a file named

R5c | S1 | shall | Store photos outside the web root.
    Verify: inspect the Nginx config and the storage

R6 | S1 | shall | Show the error "File type

   Verify: upload a wrong file type; confirm the

--- S4 CURATE ---
R13 | S4 | shall | Default display time is 45 seconds.
    The user can set 15-90 seconds per photo.
    Verify: demo + test out-of-range values  [OK]
R14 | S4 | shall | Hide keeps the photo on the device
    but out of rotation. Unhide restores it.
    Verify: demo  [OK]
R15 | S4 | shall | Remove deletes the photo from the
    device, with TRIM enabled on the photo storage

    Verify: remove a photo; confirm it is gone from
    the queue and storage; inspect mount options or
    the fstrim timer.  [OK]

ACCEPTED RISK AR-2 (MVP)
  Someone holding the SD card may recover deleted
  photos. Overwriting doesn't work on flash (wear
  leveling), and encryption doesn't help when the key
  must live on the same card for unattended boot
  (R7) and the Pi has no TPM.
  Mitigation: TRIM (R15) + disposal procedure: when
  the card is retired, physically destroy it.
  Revisit: v2 (OTP key + secure boot encryption).

R16 | S4 | shall | Shuffle can be turned on or off.
    Repeats are allowed.
    Verify: demo  [OK]

--- S5 UNAUTHORIZED ACCESS ---
R17 | S5 | shall | Require login before any upload,
    hide, remove or settings change.
    Verify: send requests without logging in  [OK]
R18 | S5 | shall | Lock out login after 4 failed
    attempts for 5 minutes.
    Verify: fail 4 logins; confirm the 5th is blocked
    even with the right password; retry after 5 min.
    [OK]
R19 | S5 | shall | End the session after 15 minutes
    with no activity, except during an upload.
    Verify: timed test  [OK]
R20 | S5 | should | Log every login and failed login
    to a file Jacob can read over SSH.
    Verify: log in, fail a login, read the log  [OK]

ACCEPTED RISK AR-1 (MVP)
  The login page runs over HTTP on the home network.
  Someone on the same Wi-Fi could capture the
  password.
  Why accepted: deadline; no self-signed certs.
  Mitigation: Eryss uses a unique password used
  nowhere else.
  Revisit: v2 (HTTPS).

--- S6 REMOTE MAINTENANCE ---
R21 | S6 | shall | SSH is reachable only through
    WireGuard.
    Verify: port scan from the LAN and the internet
    [OK]
R22 | S6 | shall | Harden SSH with these settings,
    drawn from the RHEL 9 STIG (used as a baseline,
    not a compliance claim):
    - PermitRootLogin no (V-257985)
    - PermitEmptyPasswords no (V-257984)
    - PubkeyAuthentication yes (V-257983)
    - AuthenticationMethods publickey,password
      (key AND password required)
    - ListenAddress <WireGuard IP> (enforces R21)
    - AllowUsers <admin user>
    Verify: inspect sshd_config; try root login,
    password-only login and key-only login; all must
    fail.  [OK]
